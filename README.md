## Dryer Vent Safety & Maintenance Guide

A clogged dryer vent is one of the most common — and most preventable — causes of house fires, long drying times, and early dryer failure. Lint builds up in the vent line with every cycle, restricting airflow, overheating the dryer, and wasting energy.

**Safety & maintenance basics:**

- Clean the lint screen after every load, and wash it with soap and water every few months to remove fabric-softener residue.
- Have the full dryer vent line professionally cleaned at least once a year (more often for long or bent vent runs).
- Never run the dryer while sleeping or away from home, and keep the area around it clear of boxes, clothing, and cleaning supplies.
- Check the outside vent hood regularly — it should open freely when the dryer runs and stay free of lint, nests, and plants.
- Replace plastic or foil accordion vent hose with rigid or semi-rigid metal ducting; plastic and foil traps are a fire risk.
- If clothes take more than one cycle to dry, the dryer feels very hot, or you notice a burning smell, stop using it and book a vent inspection and dryer check right away.
